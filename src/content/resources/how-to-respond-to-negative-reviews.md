Prospective customers read your replies as closely as the reviews themselves. A thoughtful response to a one-star review often does more for your reputation than another five-star one, because it shows how you behave when something goes wrong.

## Before you reply

- **Wait until you are calm.** A defensive reply lives on your profile for years.
- **Check the facts.** Look up the order, appointment or conversation so you respond to what actually happened.
- **Decide whether it breaks the rules.** If the review looks fake, comes from a competitor, contains hate speech or is about a different business, it may violate the platform's policies. Report it through the platform first (see [how to report fake reviews](/resources/how-to-report-fake-reviews/)) — and still consider a short, polite public reply.

## A simple structure

1. **Thank them.** "Thank you for taking the time to share this."
2. **Acknowledge the experience** without arguing about it. "I'm sorry your visit didn't meet expectations."
3. **Add context briefly, if it helps the next reader.** One sentence, no blame.
4. **Take it offline.** Offer a direct way to resolve it, such as a phone number or email address for a named manager.
5. **Keep it short.** Three or four sentences is plenty.

## What not to do

- Don't reveal private details about the customer, their purchase or their health, legal or financial situation.
- Don't accuse the reviewer of lying in public, even if you believe they are.
- Don't offer refunds or gifts in exchange for changing or removing a review. Many platforms prohibit it, and it can create legal problems.
- Don't copy and paste the same reply everywhere. Readers notice.

## Follow up

If you resolve the problem offline, it is fine to let the customer know they are welcome to update their review — but leave the decision entirely to them.

## Building a healthier rating

The best long-term answer to a few negative reviews is a steady stream of genuine ones. Ask every satisfied customer, make it easy with a direct link, and never filter requests so only happy customers are asked.
