## Alabama

- **Status:** None found
- **Draft page copy (review with the entry):**

  > In Alabama, booking information is often published by county sheriffs’ offices and jail rosters in places like Jefferson, Madison, Mobile and Montgomery counties, and mugshot sites copy it from there. Statewide criminal history is maintained by the Alabama Law Enforcement Agency (ALEA).
  >
  > Alabama’s main record-relief process is called expungement, and it is largely aimed at charges that did not end in a conviction. If your Alabama case was dismissed or you were found not guilty, an expungement order can be useful supporting evidence when asking websites to take a record down. A licensed Alabama attorney can tell you whether you qualify.

## Florida

- **Status:** Law identified
- **Citation:** Fla. Stat. § 901.43 (2017)
- **Summary:** Anyone in the business of publishing arrest booking photographs may not solicit or accept a fee to remove them. After a written request to the publisher’s registered agent, the photo must be removed free of charge within 10 calendar days and not republished. Courts may order removal and impose a civil penalty of $1,000 per day for noncompliance.
- **Open questions:** A 2021 bill (HB 755) proposed widening coverage to ad-funded publishers; confirm whether enacted.
- **Sources:** <https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/901.43>
- **Draft page copy (review with the entry):**

  > Florida has some of the broadest public-records rules in the country, and many sheriffs’ offices — in Miami-Dade, Broward, Hillsborough, Orange and Duval counties among others — publish booking information online, where aggregators collect it. The Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE) maintains statewide criminal history.
  >
  > Florida distinguishes between sealing and expunging a record, and both generally start with an application to FDLE before a court petition. Either can help when asking websites to remove an arrest. Because eligibility is limited, speak with a licensed Florida attorney about your record.
